{{ _("AUSVIA is intended for users 16 years of age or older. You confirm this when creating your account. We rely on your own confirmation rather than formal identity verification, consistent with how most online services handle this.") }}

{{ _("4. What data we collect") }}

  • {{ _("Account data:") }} {{ _("your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash — we never store or can see your actual password).") }}
  • {{ _("Profile data:") }} {{ _("whatever you add to your candidate profile — skills, education, location, language level, and similar information you provide to help match you with Ausbildung opportunities.") }}
  • {{ _("Documents you upload:") }} {{ _("CVs, certificates, and other files you add to your profile or applications.") }}
  • {{ _("Application data:") }} {{ _("which jobs you've applied to, the status of each application, and any documents AUSVIA generates for you (cover letters, application emails), stored so you can review and edit them.") }}
  • {{ _("Gmail data — only if you choose to connect your Gmail account:") }} {{ _("if you connect Gmail, AUSVIA requests two specific permissions from Google: permission to read your messages (used only to detect replies to applications you've sent, and only when you click \"Check for replies\" — AUSVIA does not continuously scan your inbox), and permission to create draft replies (AUSVIA can prepare a suggested reply for you to review — it never sends anything on your behalf without you personally clicking send in your own Gmail). Your Gmail access tokens are encrypted before being stored, and are never shared between users. You can disconnect Gmail at any time from your account settings, which deletes these tokens immediately.") }}
  • {{ _("Technical data:") }} {{ _("a session cookie (to keep you logged in), a language preference cookie if you're not logged in yet, and standard server logs (including IP address) used for security and abuse prevention (e.g. rate limiting).") }}

{{ _("5. Marketing emails (only if you opt in)") }}

{{ _("At registration, you can separately choose (a genuinely optional, unticked checkbox — not required to create an account) to receive occasional emails from AUSVIA about new features, tips, or updates. We only use your email for this purpose if you've actively opted in. You can withdraw this consent at any time — every marketing email includes a one-click unsubscribe link, and you can also update this preference from your account settings.") }}

{{ _("This is separate from account-related emails (like password resets), which aren't optional — they're necessary for the service to function.") }}

{{ _("6. Job posting data") }}

{{ _("AUSVIA displays job listings sourced from Germany's Federal Employment Agency (Arbeitsagentur), and, where enabled, Adzuna and Jooble. This data describes employers and job openings, not you — it's shown to you as part of the service, not collected about you.") }}

{{ _("7. AI-assisted features") }}

{{ _("AUSVIA uses AI (currently Google's Gemini; Anthropic's Claude planned) to help generate cover letters, application emails, job-match explanations, and similar content. To do this, relevant parts of your profile and the specific job posting are sent to the AI provider to generate that content. AUSVIA does not use your data to train any AI model — it's used only to generate the specific content you requested, in that moment.") }}

{{ _("8. Why we process your data (legal basis)") }}

  • {{ _("To provide the service you signed up for (matching, applications, document generation) — necessary to perform our agreement with you.") }}
  • {{ _("Marketing emails — only with your explicit, separate, opt-in consent, given at registration or later in account settings.") }}
  • {{ _("Gmail access — only with your explicit, separate consent, given when you choose to connect your account.") }}
  • {{ _("Security and abuse prevention (rate limiting, fraud prevention) — our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe and reliable.") }}

{{ _("9. Who we share data with") }}

{{ _("We don't sell your data. It's shared only with the services that make AUSVIA work:") }}

  • {{ _("Railway — hosting and database") }}
  • {{ _("Google (Gemini) — AI content generation; Google (Gmail API) — only if you connect your Gmail account") }}
  • {{ _("Anthropic — planned, once the migration from Gemini happens") }}
  • {{ _("Resend (hosted in the EU — Ireland) — sending transactional and (only if you've opted in) marketing emails") }}
